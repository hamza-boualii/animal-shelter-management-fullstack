Adoption Built on Trust & Care

We believe ethical sheltering means complete transparency. Our thorough 4-step path is designed to foster a bonded, forever match.

01

Discover & Browse

Free open sanctuary database access with honest caregiver notes, personality traits, and living requirements.

Transparent Profiles
02

Learn & Connect

Full documented veterinary history and caregiver observations regarding socialization habits and temperament.

Medical & Care Logs
03

Adoption Request

A thoughtful application evaluating lifestyle match and household needs without bureaucratic barriers.

Lifestyle Alignment
04

Sanctuary Meet & Greet

Spend unhurried time in our open sensory garden with dedicated caregivers before confirming forever adoption.
